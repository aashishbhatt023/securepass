Privacy & Limitations

Your password is analyzed locally. For breach detection, SecurePass sends only the first five characters of a SHA-1 hash to the Pwned Passwords API. The complete password and complete hash are never sent. SecurePass does not store, log, or transmit your password in any form, and never places it in a URL, query parameter, localStorage, sessionStorage, cookie, analytics event, or console log.

A “not found” breach result means the password was not present in the Pwned Passwords dataset at the time of the request — it is not a guarantee the password has never been compromised or that it is safe to use. Strength scoring is a heuristic educational estimate and cannot guarantee resistance to every attack.

SecurePass is a client-side educational project. Breach data courtesy of Have I Been Pwned. No backend, no accounts, no tracking.
